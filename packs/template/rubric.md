# Template Course Pack Rubric

This rubric is intentionally domain-neutral. Replace the wording with your course-specific criteria before publishing a real pack.

## General Scale

- 0.90-1.00: accurate, evidence-based, and usable in a new context.
- 0.75-0.89: mostly correct; minor omissions do not block the core idea.
- 0.50-0.74: partial understanding; the answer names the idea but misses a boundary, cue, or example.
- 0.00-0.49: incorrect, unsupported, or driven by a known misconception.

## Depth

- recall: states the definition or cue.
- explain: explains why the concept applies and where it stops applying.
- apply: uses the concept in a worked example.
- analyze: compares cases and identifies the changing detail.
- evaluate: judges an answer using evidence.
- create: designs a new task or artifact using the concept.
- transfer: carries the concept into a new context while preserving the right constraint.

## Evidence Rules

Grades must be evidence-bound. A grading claim should quote the learner response or task evidence exactly through the normal strict-citation path. External AI feedback may describe evidence, but it must not directly change mastery, scheduling priority, theta, or misconception state.

## Author Notes

For a real pack, replace these generic rules with course-specific examples, pass/fail boundaries, and named misconception guidance. Keep the structure stable so reviewers can audit scoring decisions.
