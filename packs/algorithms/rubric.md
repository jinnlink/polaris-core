# Algorithms Pack Rubric

## Correctness

The answer describes the algorithm, data structure, invariant, or recurrence accurately for standard inputs.

## Complexity Analysis

The answer gives time and space complexity with enough conditions to distinguish average, worst, and amortized cases when relevant.

## Boundary Awareness

The answer names at least one edge case, degeneracy, or invalid assumption that changes the algorithm's behavior.

## Transfer

The answer can map the concept to a nearby problem without hiding prerequisite constraints or changing the problem definition.
