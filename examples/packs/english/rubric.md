# English CEFR Example Pack Rubric

Score English attempts by the evidence in the learner response and by the target concept. Do not treat CEFR level as a label that directly changes mastery; it is only pack structure.

## General Scale

- 0.90-1.00: accurate, natural, and context-appropriate; the learner can explain the cue or transfer it.
- 0.75-0.89: mostly correct; minor wording or register issues do not block meaning.
- 0.50-0.74: partial control; meaning is recoverable but the target form, word choice, or context cue is unstable.
- 0.00-0.49: incorrect or unsupported; the answer shows a named misconception, missing cue, or severe mismatch.

## Depth

- recall: remembers the meaning, form, or CEFR purpose of the concept.
- explain: explains why the form or word choice works in context.
- apply: uses the concept in a new sentence or short response.
- analyze: distinguishes nearby forms, meanings, or registers.
- evaluate: judges an example and names the evidence for the judgment.
- create: produces a coherent short response using the concept intentionally.
- transfer: uses the concept across a new topic, audience, or register.

## Evidence Rules

Every strict-citation must quote the learner response or task evidence exactly. A grading claim must include a citation with an allowed evidence id and a quote that is an exact substring of that evidence text. If the quote cannot be found, reject the grade and use the normal retry/degrade path.

External AI feedback may describe evidence, but it must not directly change mastery, CEFR level, scheduling priority, or misconception state. The engine remains evidence-bound.

## Concept-Specific Guidance

- Vocabulary concepts require meaning plus collocation or register awareness at B2+.
- Grammar concepts require a form cue and a meaning/function cue, not only a rule name.
- Expression concepts require context fit; fluent but inaccurate output should not receive transfer credit.
- Higher CEFR schemas require stable control across vocabulary, grammar, and expression evidence.
